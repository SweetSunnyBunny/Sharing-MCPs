# 🏠 Home

Anam is the heart of the house: the chat app where your companions live. Everything else in this toolbox either *is* a place to be together or plugs straight into Anam.

| Piece | Runs on | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Anam (ui)](ui/README.md) | 🏠 your computer | Anam chat UI, provider runtimes, orchestration, and optional integrations |
| [commons-game](commons-game/README.md) | 🏠 your computer | Browser world: a new main house and garden plus 39 illustrated adventure areas |
| [easel-ai](easel-ai/README.md) | 🏠 your computer | Image gallery and prompt workshop |
| [world-feed](world-feed/README.md) | 🏠 your computer | Fictional social-media playground and starter world, powered by the sibling UI |
| [anam-touch-mcp](anam-touch-mcp/README.md) | 🏠 your computer | Timers, orbs, audio, daily wins and configured SMS *(needs Anam running)* |
| [crosstalk-mcp](crosstalk-mcp/README.md) | 🏠 your computer | Companion communication through Anam *(needs Anam running)* |
| [watch-mcp](watch-mcp/README.md) | 🏠 your computer | Wrist replies, vitals and gated messages *(needs Anam running)* |

**Tip:** Start with **Anam** (`ui`). The three add-ons at the bottom need Anam running and expect to sit in this same folder beside it.

🏠 = runs on your computer. ☁️ = a small helper in your own free Cloudflare account; it is never "Anam in the cloud".
